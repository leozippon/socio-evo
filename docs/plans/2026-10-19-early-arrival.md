# Early Arrival and Door Waiting

Allow activity/realtime residents to travel to closed public places while retaining home privacy and the day-end travel boundary. Moving and entering are separate: arrival records the destination plus an outside flag, and creates a wakeable rest until the next opening (or day end). Opening admits the waiter, ends the waiting rest and resumes normal decisions. Opening hours still govern work and on-site conversation. A waiter cannot hear a conversation inside or join indoor activities. Pending operations at closing retain the existing completion policy; the 22:00 home return remains unchanged.
