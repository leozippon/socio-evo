# Conversation transcript in Town Activities

The Town view's Activities panel should retain its resident links and resident-grouped activity blocks, then add a Conversations section beneath them. Selecting a conversation session shows its participants' exchanges in chronological order. A participant is someone who joined the conversation, not everyone present nearby. Person → Activities remains a resident-specific view of that resident's membership, operations and heard speech.

Conversation sessions can be grouped from the stable activity or scene identifiers already recorded in events; this needs no new publishing interface or experiment data. The transcript must stop at the replayed time and respect the current truth or selected-reader visibility rules, including who could hear each exchange. Resumed activity membership remains distinct where the existing replay model treats it as a new interval.

This change belongs in the static viewer and its documentation. It does not change experiments or their published data. Validation should cover grouping participants and exchanges by session, chronological ordering, replay-time truncation, truth/reader visibility, and preservation of resident-specific details and links.
