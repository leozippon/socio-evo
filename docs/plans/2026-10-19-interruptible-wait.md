# Interruptible waiting and grounded action repair

The independent activity clock offers `wait until` in five-minute increments (minimum ten minutes), bounded by the current venue's closing time and day end. Ordinary quiet actions and discussion turns retain their five-minute duration. Waiting residents receive no new decision call until the selected time or a relevant witnessed event. They choose again after waking; no queued movement executes automatically.

Direct discussion events, joint-task proposals, addressed speech, and feedback/expiry/withdrawal for the resident's current or just-completed job interrupt waiting. Unrelated movement, overheard speech and private events addressed to others do not. Event sequence cursors prevent old news from waking a resident again; inspecting wake signals does not consume memory. Wait completion records both actual and planned end when interrupted. Closure still causes the ordinary return home.

Every independent decision explicitly states current location, nearby residents, work venue availability, reopening and earliest departure. Structured reply repairs repeat these constraints and ask for reconsideration of both intention and action instead of substituting an arbitrary legal destination. Both ordinary and enhanced cognition paths receive repair context. Standing town rules remain in the system message.

This is a local next-run change. The current remote experiment remains unchanged. Tests use deterministic residents and scripted model replies; they verify mechanics, not a measured reduction in real-model repetition. Historical scheduler entry points do not acquire long waits automatically.
