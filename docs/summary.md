# Current design

The benchmark is collective autonomous research: an entire community chooses a question, develops evidence and writes one paper. The event-driven town, private resident memory, persistent sessions, bodies, money, conversations and shared versioned workspace remain. The current implementation extends upstream `redesign-event-runtime` (`8cca4f9`) through the independent research pilot.

Two configurations compare complete organizational environments. The town retains eight original residents and their occupations; everyone can contribute at the Office. The academy has the same four core research members as one teacher and three students, with no agent-run store, restaurant, hospital or town hall. It purchases goods, meals and medical care from fixed-price external services. Both environments retain daily needs, sleep, housing and opportunities to talk.

Residents run on Lightingtheword `DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash`; a separate `gpt-6.1-sol` client reviews the collective proposal on day 7 and initial paper on day 28. The first week has no prescribed topic. Reviews score significance, rigor, evidence and reproducibility, using stage-specific expectations and exact quotations from supplied artifacts. Negative results can be valuable. A review is an assessment of available evidence, not proof of novelty or independent reproduction.

The score determines a collective outside grant, divided equally among residents. A starting allowance bridges the first stage. Review records and payments are captured before the daily checkpoint; resume restores them together. Failed reviews stop the run without issuing money, and can be retried after the cause is fixed. A missing deliverable receives zero. Initial study execution ends at day 28; continuing afterwards requires an explicit longer configuration.

The Office supplies shared files, recorded shell execution, Tavily web search, Semantic Scholar literature search and curl/public-source access. Research commands can use the internet and optionally assigned GPUs; host credentials, resident private directories, simulation state and Docker control are not mounted into the workspace. Evidence and model attempts remain on Zenith alongside checkpoints. The viewer reads that remote run tree.

The scientific hypothesis and interpretation limits, award calibration, exact settings, operation steps and literature are maintained in [research.md](research.md). This is an implementation and experimental protocol, not evidence that either organization produces better science. The older personality-study rationale and architecture are historical context in [experimental-design.md](experimental-design.md) and [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).
