# Current design

The current protocol is a four-person AI institute. Mei, Aisha, Ravi and Kofi are doctoral students with different backgrounds. They retain private memories, conversations, personalities, bodies, sleep, housing and money. Fixed-price external shops, meals and healthcare replace agent service occupations.

The first seven complete simulated days are ordinary life, without an announced common research objective, deadline or review reward. At 07:00 on day 8, a common announcement opens the Office research facilities and asks them to choose an AI question together, aiming for work suitable for leading computer-science conferences such as ICML and ICLR. They submit a LaTeX manuscript every 21 research days. This run plans three reviews, on days 28, 49 and 70; longer horizons can be configured before launch.

Residents use local Qwen3.8-27B-FP8 on host GPU 0. Recorded research shell calls use host GPU 1, exposed inside the CUDA/PyTorch container as `cuda:0`. The announcement and help provide explicit detection and training examples. The container also has LaTeX compilation tools. Shared study files and execution evidence are versioned; dependencies and model caches persist separately. Tavily and curl are available after the announcement; Semantic Scholar is disabled.

The external `gpt-6.1-sol` API reviewer returns a simulated conference review: technical and novelty assessments, an overall score, confidence, strengths, weaknesses, questions and required revisions. Its score determines an equal collective grant. All residents receive feedback and the next deadline, then decide whether to revise or select another question. Manuscripts, exact inputs, reviews and execution evidence are retained; checkpoint restoration prevents duplicate awards.

The new run started on November 15, 2026 at 01:21 Beijing time and is available through the [forwarded viewer](http://127.0.0.1:8921/research.html?run=ai-institute-seed-20260302). The [research protocol](research.md) defines the calendar, funding and evidence limits; the [operations guide](../ops/research/README.md) owns deployment status and commands. The earlier teacher/student laboratory and completed town/academy pair remain separate historical experiments. These changes in roles, resources, funding and feedback prevent treating their differences as a controlled causal comparison.
