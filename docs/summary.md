# Current design

The active experiment is a model-enabled continuation of an AI information-retrieval-agent laboratory at Beijing Zhongguancun Academy, used as a simulated institutional setting. Four doctoral peers retain private memories, conversations, personalities, bodies, sleep, housing and money. Its background readings are correctly attributed external research, not invented achievements of the group.

The calendar starts on March 1, 2026 and plans 306 days. The first seven complete lived days are free; at 07:00 on March 8 the group receives its common research commission. Residents choose the question and use an Office browser for dated arXiv versions and GitHub commits. Qwen runs on host GPU 0 and scientific commands on host GPU 1, exposed as `cuda:0`. The research container has no direct internet; papers and code enter through the dated browser, while a [model library](model-library.md) supplies pinned Hugging Face snapshots. Residents can load admitted weights with Transformers or request recorded vLLM generation on GPU 1. Original model weights stay outside research Git.

An explicit conference desk replaces automatic 21-day paper reviews: registration, frozen LaTeX/PDF submission, three reviews, author response, ARR commitment where applicable, updated reviews, final decision and camera-ready delivery. Different papers may be submitted in parallel, including at the same venue; each submission ID has its own feedback, deadlines and grant. Waiting for a decision does not prevent further research. The reviewer remains `gpt-6.1-sol`. Living support arrives independently every 21 days; conference decisions can provide an equal additional grant. The calendar identifies official sources and simulated dates. Decisions beyond the observation window remain pending.

This is a dated-source experiment, not a guarantee that model knowledge or current search indexes contain no future information. Submission/commit timestamps do not prove historical public availability. The [IR laboratory protocol](ir-laboratory.md) defines the design, expected evidence and limits; the [operations guide](../ops/research/README.md) owns deployment and acceptance. The [active viewer](http://127.0.0.1:8923/research.html?run=ir-lab-models-seed-20260302) follows the November 16 continuation from complete checkpoint 20. The [original IR trajectory](http://127.0.0.1:8922/research.html?run=ir-lab-pit-seed-20260302), including subsequent day-21 activity, remains preserved. The continuation inherits memories, scientific history and conference state; model access is an explicit mid-trajectory resource change, not a restart of free life.

The previous 70-day institute experiment is stopped, retaining its day-27 checkpoint and later day-28 live records, frozen configuration, [21-day review protocol](research.md) and remote records. Its [viewer](http://127.0.0.1:8921/research.html?run=ai-institute-seed-20260302) has separate original-first, second and third manuscript PDF entries. The earlier teacher/student laboratory and completed town/academy pair remain historical experiments. These changed conditions do not form a controlled causal comparison.
