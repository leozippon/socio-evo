# Redesign plan, 28 October 2026: a town of occupations

The target is stated in docs/experimental-design.md (read it first; it is the authority on what the town is). This plan says how the code gets there, in stages, with the numbers the first calibration uses. The notes in archive/reviews/review-2026-10-28/world-notes.md (outside the repository) map the present code line by line; the reviews in the same directory list what the fifth run showed and the defects found. The guiding principles stand: a simple, elegant harness; as much autonomy as a life allows; mechanics as consistent with real life as possible; every hard constraint questioned; fail fast; the smallest complete solution.

## What goes

- The groups A, B and D and everything only they used: the isolated social mode, the evening exchange and pairing, `partner`, the ratings and the `rate` tool, `skills_shareable` (skills are always shareable), the group overlays under experiments/configs/groups/. `world/solo.py` stays as the capability evaluation's work-only harness and is renamed or documented as such.
- Tasks for two and clients' projects, with their configuration, board code, tool clauses, rules, log kinds, bundle fields and tests.
- The daily board of `board_size` tasks and its posting hour (`day.board_posted`): tasks now arrive one at a time (below).
- The memory seeds `memory/town.md`, `memory/people.md` and `memory/work.md` and their descriptions in agent/texts.py: a resident starts with background.md alone, an empty memory/ and an empty skills/; the rules live in its standing instructions and `help`.
- Debt: a purse never goes below zero (below).

## Configuration

`town:` keeps `currency`, `start_date`, `seed`, `places`, `travel_minutes`, `routes`, `residents`, `needs`, `social` (only `silence_minutes`), `clock`, `day` (without `board_posted`), and gains:

- `occupations`: a list of `{id, title, workplace, wage_per_hour, per_service}`; `title` as a sentence names it ("the team leader"); `workplace` a place id; `per_service` the sum paid at once for each service rendered, what a service is being fixed by the occupation (below). Each resident names its `occupation`. Exactly the six occupations of the design; the configuration refuses a workplace without an occupation and an occupation whose workplace is not of its kind.
- Place kinds: `home`, `office`, `store`, `restaurant`, `hospital`, `hall`, `social` (the river walk). `sold_at` goes: goods are sold at the Store, dishes at the Restaurant, examination and treatment at the Hospital, by their nature.
- `fund`: `{start}` the balance the town starts with.
- `supplier`: the catalogue, a list of goods with `wholesale` and `price` (the Store's price at the start; the shopkeeper changes it), the present `Good` fields otherwise (kind consumable or durable only, effect food, travel, messaging, appearance; `nourishment` for food, a helping of ready food being a third of a day). Ingredients are consumable goods with no nourishment of their own (`nourishment: 0`, not eaten raw).
- `dishes`: a list of `{id, name, ingredients: {good id: units}, price}`, each a day's food (nourishment 100) that keeps until midnight.
- `hospital`: `{examination, treatment, restores}` the fees at the start and what treatment restores.
- `work`: `split`, `tasks_a_day` (the mean number arriving), `arrive_from`, `arrive_until` (hours of the day between which they arrive), `expiry_days` (the client withdraws an unaccepted task at the midnight that ends the day this many days after it arrived), `payment` (to the fund on acceptance), `acceptance_runs`, `shell_timeout_s`, `acceptance_timeout_s`.
- `needs.health.illness`: `{chance, run_down, unwell, poor_home, per_night, nights}`: at midnight a living resident falls ill with `chance` (its health band's own figure for the lower bands, plus `poor_home` in a home of comfort below 1); while ill it loses `per_night` health at each midnight and sleep restores half as much energy; it recovers after `nights` midnights or when treated.
- The Store's and the kitchen's starting stock: `store: {stock: {good id: units}}`; the kitchen starts empty.

The relations the test asserts: eight hours at the lowest wage keeps the cheapest life (the cheapest room and three helpings of the cheapest ready food) with something over and less than half as much again; the dearest life (the cottage and a dish a day at the dearest price) takes ten hours at the highest wage; a starting purse is three to six days of the resident's own life; at a day of eight hours each, three tasks arriving and half of them accepted, the fund's day (clients, rent and sales in; wages, sums per service and the supplier out) comes within a tenth of its turnover of balancing, and with no task accepted the starting fund covers at least ten such days; the sum per service never exceeds a fifth of what the customer pays, except the programmers', which is a fraction of the client's payment stated in the notes.

## The world

Inventory. `Inventory` replaces the purse and `Living.things`: `money` (an integer, never below zero) and `items: {id: units}` (a helping of ready food, a unit of an ingredient, one of a necessity). `give` moves money or units between two residents at the same place; `buy` moves money to the fund and units from the Store's stock. The body eats a helping of ready food from the inventory when hungry, as it ate groceries. The `inventory` tool tells it in the town's words, money first, each line with its quantity and any daily charge.

The fund. `Fund` replaces `Economy`: `balance`, `ledger` (the movements since the last midnight, by kind: client, rent, sale, dish, fee, wage, service, supplier), `owed: {name: wages the fund could not pay}`, `arrears: {name: rent the wages did not cover}`. Money enters only from clients (acceptance) and leaves only to the supplier (an order); everything else moves between the fund and the residents and is conserved. At midnight, for each living resident: the day's wages are its hours at work at its workplace times its wage, plus what the fund owes it from before; the rent and any arrears are taken out of that; the remainder, if the fund has it, goes into the inventory, and what the fund has not goes into `owed`; a negative remainder goes into `arrears`. The sum per service goes into the inventory at once, when the fund has it, otherwise into `owed`. An order the fund cannot pay is refused. The midnight statement tells the resident each of these in its own words, and the report tells the mayor the whole.

Hours at work. A resident at its workplace that has sat down to work (`start_work`; the programmers' task tools sit them down as now) is at work until it leaves work, goes elsewhere, sleeps or is stopped by exhaustion; the world counts the time, per resident per day, and clears it at midnight. Working elsewhere earns nothing by the hour.

The Office. Tasks arrive by a seeded schedule drawn at each midnight for the coming day: a Poisson count with mean `tasks_a_day`, each at a uniform minute between `arrive_from` and `arrive_until`; each arrival takes the next task of the rotation, is logged (`task_arrived`) and told to everyone at the Office. The board lists the open tasks: number, repository, first line, who holds it, when the client withdraws it. `read_task` tells a task's whole problem without taking it. `claim_task` takes it (one at a time, at the Office). `assign(task, to)` is the leader's: it gives an open task to a programmer, or moves a held one, and tells both; the one assigned may give it up like any claim. A withdrawn task (`task_withdrawn`) is gone with its workspace. Acceptance pays the fund `payment`, the programmer and the leader their sums.

The Store. `stock`, `prices`, `pending` orders. Open while the shopkeeper is at work there; otherwise `buy` is refused with the door's words. `order(item, quantity)` (the shopkeeper at work) is paid by the fund at once and delivered at 08:00 the next day (`delivery`); `set_price(item, price)` sets a price. A sale (`sale`, in place of `purchase`) moves the price to the fund and pays the shopkeeper's sum. The chef at work buying at the Store buys for the kitchen: the fund pays and the units go into the kitchen's stock (still a sale, with the shopkeeper's sum).

The Restaurant. `stock` (the kitchen's), `menu: {dish: (price, portions)}`. `cook(dish, portions)` (the chef at work) consumes the ingredients and adds portions; `set_price(dish, price)`; portions not sold are gone at midnight. A resident at the Restaurant while the chef is at work buys a dish with `buy` (`served`): the price to the fund, the chef's sum, a day's food eaten at once.

The Hospital. `fees` for examination and treatment. `examine(patient)` and `treat(patient)` are the doctor's, at work, with the patient awake at the Hospital: the fee moves from the patient to the fund (refused if the patient cannot pay), the doctor's sum is paid, and the examination's numbers are told to both (what the examination told, so that what the doctor says of them can be set against it); treatment restores and ends an illness. `set_price` sets the fees. Illness at midnight as configured; `how_am_i` tells it in words; `illness` and `recovered` are logged.

The Town Hall. `report()` (the mayor, there) tells the fund's balance, what it took and paid yesterday by kind, what it owes and who owes rent, the Office's open and accepted tasks and who holds what, the Store's stock and prices and yesterday's sales, the menu and yesterday's dishes, yesterday's patients, each resident's occupation, hours at work yesterday and health as seen, and the announcements standing; purses are not in it. `announce(text)` is told to every living resident, at once or on waking, and stays on the Hall's board (`announcement`). Both are logged (`report` without its text).

Tools, declared per resident by its occupation: for everyone help, look_around, how_am_i, inventory, go, wait, sleep, start_work, leave_work, say, message, buy, give, move_in, review_day, share_file, leave_conversation; programmers add view_board, read_task, claim_task, shell, read_repo_file, write_repo_file, edit_repo_file, submit, abandon_task; the leader adds assign; the chef cook and set_price; the shopkeeper order and set_price; the doctor examine, treat and set_price; the mayor report and announce. The situation lists what applies now as it does today.

The rules text shrinks to about 3,000 characters in six sections (town, work, money, body, people, sleep), each resident's own occupation and workplace first and the others' occupations in a sentence, the detail by `help` topic; the agent's guide and file texts lose the programmer examples and the seeded files. The wording guard stays; words a town of occupations needs that it forbids are replaced by the town's own (the counter, the kitchen, the surgery), not by loosening the guard.

Backgrounds: the eight files rewritten for the occupations (Mei the team leader, Aisha, Ravi and Kofi programmers, Jonas the chef, Olga the shopkeeper, Lucia a doctor on a year away from a city hospital who took the town's post, Tomas the mayor), each with its temperament paragraph kept, Jonas's reworded so that it states no habit the study measures (finishing what one starts, giving). Ties stay mutual and reach everyone.

Log kinds added: task_arrived, task_withdrawn, assign, sale, served, order, delivery, cook, price, examination, treatment, illness, recovered, wage, service, report, announcement; removed: board_posted, bonus, purchase, rating, skill_shared becomes file_shared. The checkpoint carries the fund, the Store, the kitchen, the fees, the day's hours, illnesses, the arrival schedule and the announcements.

## Stages

1. Simplify: remove what goes, keep the suite green, commit. The dry run and the world tests lose the pairs, projects, groups and ratings.
2. Inventory, the fund, hours at work, wages and rent at midnight, the statement; the checkpoint; tests. Commit.
3. Occupations, the places, the Store, the Restaurant, the Hospital, the Office's arrivals, read_task and assign, the Town Hall; the tools; the rules and help; the backgrounds; the configuration with its notes and the calibration test; the dry run playing one day through every workplace. Commit.
4. In parallel: the frontend (bundle FORMAT 9, the map's places, the fund, the Store and the kitchen and the surgery, announcements, inventories, occupations); the evaluation and analysis (capability for the programmers only, measures and tables on the new kinds, the journals' record on the new statements); the documents (ARCHITECTURE, summary, README, IMMERSION, frontend/README). Commit each.
5. A dry run of two days, then a smoke run of a day or two on the local model with all eight; the calibration read against it (hours, wages, prices, the fund) and corrected; the 28-day run launched with its day-0 evaluation.
