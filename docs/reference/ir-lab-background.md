# IR laboratory setting and source provenance

The new laboratory is an experimental simulation. Its four residents and their affiliation with an AIcore information-retrieval group at Beijing Zhongguancun Academy are assigned roles, not claims about real academy members, an actual laboratory, institutional endorsement or the academy's research output. Resident backgrounds state these roles naturally; this operator-facing document records their status.

The authoritative reading packet is [the laboratory background resource](../../experiments/resources/ir-lab-background.md). It is expanded into the frozen configuration, supplied as private reading notes at creation and copied to the shared library at the research announcement. Opening biographies contain doctoral identity, experience and social history. Neither the biographies nor the historical reading notes disclose the future collective commission or conference calendar.

The institutional context is grounded in the academy's [official autumn-camp notice dated 18 September 2025](https://www.bza.edu.cn/detail/slfyipu0nro57856gvqel2y5fzf62rgw), which identifies AIcore directions in algorithms, models, systems and embodied intelligence. It does not identify this simulated group. The reading packet's three papers belong to their cited external authors. Their arXiv version histories were checked on 15 November 2026: [Search-R1 v5, 5 August 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.09516v5), [DeepResearcher v4, 17 April 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.03160v4), and [WebThinker v2, 13 October 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21776v2). All precede the configured 1 March 2026 start.

The simulation calendar and the version cutoff are separate from historical availability. A current search index, an arXiv submission date or a Git commit date does not prove what a user could discover at that historical moment. Gateway provenance must retain this limitation. Contemporary resident-model weights can contain later knowledge; restricting retrieved versions does not establish that the model's internal knowledge is historically isolated.

The 306-day configuration covers March through December 2026. As of the verification date, its final weeks have not happened. The launcher therefore stops at the latest daily checkpoint already reached in real Beijing time and leaves the same frozen run available for a later resume. It must not manufacture future papers, repository history or conference decisions.

Research containers have no direct network access. Data, dependencies and weights must already exist in the image or arrive through permitted, recorded downloads; a new experiment does not inherit the previous experiment's runtime cache. A successful CUDA optimizer probe establishes available compute, not the availability of every desired information-retrieval dataset or training checkpoint. Resident choices may include lightweight retrieval experiments built from accessible material, without being confined to a prescribed question.
