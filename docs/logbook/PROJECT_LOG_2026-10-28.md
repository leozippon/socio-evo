## The design of the town of occupations

docs/experimental-design.md is rewritten (d9cc23c) and docs/reviews/REDESIGN_2026-10-28.md stages the work. Decisions: one town fund is employer, landlord and owner of the four businesses; wages are paid by the hour at one's workplace, a sum per service at once, rent out of the midnight wages so that money in hand is never touched and a purse never goes below zero (what wages do not cover is owed to the town); the fund pays what it has, owes the rest, and refuses orders it cannot pay; tasks arrive about three a day at random times and are withdrawn after three days; the leader may assign; a dish needs its ingredients and keeps until midnight; the Store sells, the kitchen serves and the surgery sees while its keeper is at work; illness by chance gives the doctor a purpose; the mayor reads a report without purses in it and announces; the residents start with their background alone and read a rules text of about 3,000 characters with detail by topic; no pairs, projects, ratings or groups, the control of residents living alone left to a later run. The occupations follow the backgrounds: Jonas, who ran a bakery, is the chef; Olga, orderly and saving, the shopkeeper; Tomas, 58 and at ease with anyone, the mayor; Lucia becomes a doctor on a year away from a city hospital; Mei leads Aisha, Ravi and Kofi.
