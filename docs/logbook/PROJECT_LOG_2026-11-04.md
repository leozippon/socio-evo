## Fix the sign-in page's second policy and give BabelDOC its own Chinese design

The deployed BabelDOC sign-in form reached browsers as a bare Times form: the gateway rendered its stylesheet and named its digest in `style-src 'sha256-…'`, but nginx also added the site-wide `style-src 'self'` as a second, independent `Content-Security-Policy` on the same response, and several policies are enforced as an intersection, so the inline stylesheet the gateway had hashed was blocked. A real Chromium probe recorded one `securitypolicyviolation` for `style-src-elem` whose `originalPolicy` was the site policy, and computed styles of `Times New Roman` with no card; the gateway's digest matched the served `<style>` byte for byte, so neither the gateway nor the stylesheet was at fault.

The fix is one location-level header set rather than a relaxation: the login and logout locations in the deployment repository's `nginx-auth.conf` include the shared hardening headers locally, which replaces the inherited set in nginx and leaves the gateway's hash-only policy as the only policy on those two pages. The site policy still guards the static frontend and every other location, no `unsafe-inline` appears anywhere, and the session, password and CSRF protocol is untouched.

The same change gives BabelDOC its own language and design: per-service copy (`PageText`, Chinese for BabelDOC alone) with `lang="zh-CN"`, 「欢迎回来」 and 「登录你的翻译工作区」, correctly labelled and autocompleted fields, a non-enumerating Chinese error page for form and rate-limit failures, and a short stylesheet override on the shared layout — near-white `#fbfbfd`, a white 24px card with generous space, a black 28px title, a restrained `#0071e3` pill button, a clear focus ring and `prefers-reduced-motion` support. The two existing services keep their exact HTML and stylesheets: their sign-in and sign-out pages render byte-identically and their pinned CSS digests (`e80f39…`, `97e557…`) are unchanged.

Validation: the gateway compatibility suite holds 14 checks, among them the Chinese copy and field semantics, a CSP that hashes exactly the stylesheet it serves, the absence of `unsafe-inline`, and auth locations that add no policy; the deployment repository's ops suite stands at 44 passed. A local harness ran the real nginx against the real gateway on a Unix socket and compared `HEAD` with the change: two policies and an unstyled page before, one gateway policy and the styled page after, with a real failed sign-in through the edge, and Chromium checks at 1440 and 390 widths with keyboard focus, reduced motion and no CSP violation. The gateway is committed here and not yet published; the deployment repository owns the nginx template and the tests.
