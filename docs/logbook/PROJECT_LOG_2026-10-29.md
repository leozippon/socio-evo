## The first day reviewed; the reorganisation brought forward

The smoke run lived its day in 36 real minutes without a traceback, a lapse, a cut-off reply or a tool error, with 81% of prompt tokens served from cache and three clean compactions; the money added up to the dollar; 61 things were said in 18 conversations, 30 of them at the Linde, and residents met at meals and at the Store. The review (archive/reviews/review-2026-10-29/smoke-day1.md) found what the day got wrong. Defects: a call to a tool the request had not declared was answered "There is nothing you can do called X" instead of being refused with the reason, so a greeting could cancel a purchase and a resident in a conversation was told its own tools did not exist, which left Aisha unfed all day, Ravi paying twice for one omelette, Kofi at zero hours and in arrears, Mei without the task she had tried to claim and Olga without her bed or journal; the shopkeeper could not see the Store's stock and ordered $337 onto full shelves; a resident put to sleep at 04:00 was still asked to act. Calibration: the fund fell $332 against the notes' +$42, mostly the over-ordering and two residents' unpaid rent; the mayor worked 1.3 hours because his duty held only at the Hall; the doctor earned the most for no patients. Design limitations: a conversation offered no buying, a chef could not treat anyone to a dish, the report covered one day. Site: the town feed styled wakings, conditions and sitting down to work as public although every `wake` is told to the waker alone; a purchase did not say where it was made (in the data every one of the chef's was at the Store).

The user's directions on reading the list: rebalance the occupations' incomes for a sustainable economy; every resident pays taxes, and the taxes fund the mayor's salary; abstract every item into one class held at a specific location (the Store, an inventory), so that shopping and transfers are real, with operations such as get and pass; and, in short, build the whole system on clean, well-designed abstractions. The reorganisation is therefore brought forward (docs/reviews/REDESIGN_2026-10-28.md, stage 7): items held by exactly one holder each (a resident, a place, the supplier) with one move function enforcing presence, the tools get, pass, put, eat and buy, the refusal fix, the stock visible to its keeper, a conversation that allows shopping, the mayor's duty running on anywhere; then a tax of a tenth on wages and sums into a tax account that pays the mayor, and rates of 7 an hour for all with sums of 30 a task, 15 for the leader, 2 a dish, 1 a sale, 8 a patient; then the site's privacy styling; then the rest of the code and the clutter; a second smoke run; its review; the launch. The smoke run is archived (archive/runs/smoke-2026-10-29) and the publisher stopped until the next run.
