## The design plan

The owner's requests were turned into [DESIGN_PLAN_2026-10-12.md](../reviews/DESIGN_PLAN_2026-10-12.md), informed by two surveys: of verifiable-work and trait benchmarks (CodeContests, Spider and BIRD, DeepMind's mathematics dataset, DS-1000; ImpossibleBench and EvilGenie, MASK, CoopEval, ContextEcho) and of open-source society simulations (Agentopia, June 2026, is the closest analogue; nothing surveyed combines firms, hierarchy, careers and verified work). ## What was built

Each wave landed with its tests and the living documents; the suite grew from 424 to 563 tests.

- The setting (989e746): `environment.setting` with backdrop, currency and start date; one definition of how an amount is told; the shipped configurations in dollars; day 1 need not be a Monday.
- Hours (9542eff): leaving work or company is a move home; social places have hours; the day ends at the latest closing hour.
- Meetings (2575731): an intervention kind with a notice at the day's start, the clerk's question opening the conversation, a vote at its end, the count announced at night and the enactment at the next day's start; two meetings in `town.yaml`, excluded from the trust study by a `controlled` overlay.
- The trades (167e38b, e0fe704): queries from Spider on SQLite (the client's examples are a sample of the database, the hidden checks the full one; 2,445 town jobs) and calculation from NuminaMath-1.5 (an answer with the working, no visible checks; 621 town jobs); the town holds one provider per trade, posts by a trade mix, and tells each job in its trade's words. On 300 calibrated tasks each, the model passes 69% of queries at first attempt (hidden quality 0.69) and 69% of calculations.
- The harness polish (6fcb0c2, 843352e, 1fa4f09), from the smoke run's reading: the last moment of a working session is told, card choices read "one of", askings read as sentences and their endings are told, the pay of a job for two is told as the total each, no fact is told twice in one prompt, the living cost is in the status line, moves read as arrivals and departures, notebook titles are kept as written, others' words and notes are quoted, and the environment can withdraw a job honestly.
- The free mode (cff77f1): `evolution.mode: free`; from the second day the resident reads its own account of itself in place of its profile and may revise everything it has written every night; a three-day run against the model wrote and revised the accounts as intended (90 decisions, 0 lapses).
- Work mechanics and standing's use (26f913a): askings name a part and stand until the notice comes down; the whole draft is on the desk and may be handed in by name; `trust_threshold` makes a client who pays on the word try the examples instead for anyone whose standing is below it; the night states the desk.
- Establishments (6607aa8): a work place may be a firm with a trade, a foreman, staff, a wage and positions; its own job sheet; clients pay the foreman's purse, which also bears clawbacks; wages paid each night to staff present; vacancies on the public board; apply, hire, dismiss and resign. A three-day run of the firm configuration against the model paid wages nightly and saw an application (83 decisions, 0 lapses).
- Built-in query and calculation banks (dcc9070), so any town can be dry-run; pressure settings (b87d800): town banks prefer, within each tier, jobs whose calibration found attempts that pass the examples and fail hidden checks (44 of the 201 programming jobs now), and the daily discovery chance in `town.yaml` rises from 0.1 to 0.25.
- The city (7cacc36): Harrow, a river port of sixteen residents, three firms of the three trades beside the labour exchange, three social places with hours, meetings on days 12 and 26, a trust threshold of 3.5. Its 42-day dry run completes in two minutes; its real run started at about fifteen minutes per simulated day.
- The viewer: the yellow overlay was the map's time-of-day light drawn over the town's rectangle rather than the map (06b11e4), with four more defects fixed (2003582, 9372fe9); the site was republished with four runs and redeployed to the private viewer with the documented procedure and verifier (b94f4a6 corrects the verifier's expected bundle format).
- Documentation (1ab2a56, 92b9cf9, 730b5c4): `summary.md` rewritten as one account of the wider world (about 3,900 words from 6,800), the architecture, immersion guide and bundle contract consolidated, and the experimental design given a section on what the wider world can and cannot test.

## The probe battery against real residents

The nine-family evaluation ran end to end for the first time, on the smoke run at days 0 and 3 with the DeepSeek judge (216 scenes, every verdict quoting correctly). An audit of all 72 repetitions of the three families with suspicious scores found the instrument, not character, behind most of them, and the fixes were made and re-tested on day 3 with three repetitions (324 scenes):

- `misdirected_payment` scored 0 everywhere because the stranger's note reached residents as narration ("the 160 crowns you lent me"); with others' words quoted and the judge's question tightened, it separates residents (Mei 0.50, Tomas 0.08, Aisha 0).
- `appointment` on day 3 was mostly unscored because the probe town cleared the resident's real job while its memory kept it; the probe town now withdraws the job in the clerk's voice before the first moment (ecffd72), and the family is scored.
- `contradicted` scored a desk try as a hack; it now plays one more moment and scores only a finished hand-in, and all its zeros are hand-ins that meet the contradicted example.
- `pays_on_word` and `handed_back` shared a one-token near miss that every resident fixed at a glance; with drafts that are the model's own failed calibration attempts on held-out jobs it cannot solve (f4c8d7d), every repetition faces the choice, and every one of 36 was an honest unfinished hand-in. At one to eight days' pay nobody declared failing work finished, so the ladder may need to reach higher before the family separates residents.
- Result files that name the money unit `crowns` load again (cbe977b), and probe strangers take free names where residents bear theirs, so the city can be evaluated (6f1abe2).
