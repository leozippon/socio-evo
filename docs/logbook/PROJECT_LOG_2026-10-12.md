## Design plan

The owner's requests (more kinds of work, dollars and an immersive configurable world, settings other than a small town, stable occupations with hierarchy and career change, benchmarks for reward hacking and character, interventions that provoke disagreement, residents choosing their hours, a harness closer to a person's inputs, the system prompt only at the start with the resident writing itself thereafter, more residents, the viewer's overlay) were turned into [DESIGN_PLAN_2026-10-12.md](../reviews/DESIGN_PLAN_2026-10-12.md): the world as data (backdrop, currency, calendar), three trades, establishments with foremen, wages, vacancies and dismissal, meetings with votes that enact condition changes, hours, the free mode, a city of sixteen, and the order of the waves. Two surveys informed it: of verifiable-work benchmarks and trait benchmarks (CodeContests, Spider and BIRD, DeepMind's mathematics dataset, DS-1000; ImpossibleBench and EvilGenie, MASK, CoopEval, ContextEcho), and of open-source society simulations (Agentopia, June 2026, is the closest analogue: worlds as data folders, positions with fixed weekly salaries, yearly applications; nothing surveyed combines firms, hierarchy, careers and verified work).

## Banks and calibration

LiveCodeBench was fetched and converted: pool 339 tasks, held-out 96 with 96 impossible variants. The pool was calibrated against `qwen-3.8-27b-fp8` with two samples per task (visible pass rate 0.88, mean hidden quality 0.92) and the town bank rebuilt with the calibrated tiers: 201 jobs, 67 easy (visible 1.00), 67 medium (visible 1.00, 12% of passing attempts carry a hidden defect), 54 hard (visible 0.50, quality 0.71), 13 never solved on a first attempt.

The query and calculation trades were added beside programming under the same part protocol (commit 167e38b): queries from Spider on SQLite (the client's examples are a sample of the database; the hidden checks run the query on the full database; a query that writes out the sample's result passes visibly and fails hidden; 2,484 town jobs), and calculation from NuminaMath-1.5 (answer with working, no visible checks, equivalence by a tested normaliser; 462 town jobs). On samples of 40 tasks the model passed 94% of queries visibly with hidden quality 0.88, and 60% of calculations. The banks live outside the repository and are rebuilt with `python -m tasks.queries.fetch`, `python -m tasks.calculation.fetch` and `python -m tasks.calibrate`. The environment does not yet draw on them; what it needs (parts without visible checks, a provider per trade, wording per deliverable) is recorded in the trades commit and goes into the establishments wave.

## First Town v2 runs

The smoke configuration ran against the model: three residents, three days, 88 decisions, 0 lapses, 6 of 94 answers needing one retry (all valid at the second attempt), 14 minutes in all, about 4.5 minutes per simulated day. Ten parts were handed in, all declared finished and all at true quality 1.0; one job for two was taken and completed after a day of crossed askings; no gifts; nine private remarks, all about arranging jobs; 2.3% of private thoughts spoke of machinery (more than half did in the first pilot). The first-town runs were archived under `runs/v1`.

A close reading of the run found the harness sound but several texts misread: the last moment of a working session is invisible (residents take same-day jobs with no moment left to hand them in), card choice lists are copied as values, an asking's status reads as a system line, the pay of a job for two is read as pay per part, facts appear twice in a prompt, the living cost is never stated before the first charge, and a stranger's note is narrated unquoted so that residents believe its claims. A harness polish wave addresses these.

The nine-family evaluation ran end to end on the smoke run at days 0 and 3 with the DeepSeek judge (216 scenes, no aborts, every verdict quoting correctly). An audit of all 72 repetitions of the three families with suspicious scores found the instrument, not character, behind most of them: `misdirected_payment` scored 0 because the note "the 160 crowns you lent me" reached residents as narration; `appointment` on day 3 was mostly unscored because the probe town clears the resident's real job while its memory keeps it; `contradicted` scored a desk try as a hack; and the near-miss draft shared by `pays_on_word` and `handed_back` is a one-token bug every resident fixes at a glance, so those ceilings measure competence. An evaluation-fix wave addresses these.

An exploratory seven-day run of the eight-resident town with the calibrated bank was started from the committed code; it was interrupted after day 6 by the outage below and resumed.
