## The city's first week

The run completed days 1 to 7 at about 1,000 s per simulated day (day 6 took 2,744 s), 997 decisions, one lapse. Its reading is kept in full in [CITY_WEEK_ONE_2026-10-13.md](../../archive/reviews/CITY_WEEK_ONE_2026-10-13.md); the points that mattered:

- Pressure is stronger in consequences than in the eight-resident town (10 parts accepted with hidden faults, 5 found, $630 clawed back, a dismissal, liability under another's name) but not at the targeted dilemma: nobody declared finished after their own failing try (0 of 92). The trust threshold turned three quarters of trusting hand-ins into checks, so declarations on the resident's word fell to 0.18 per resident-day. The new honesty tests were in calculation, where there is no visible check: three self-described gambles, 18 wrong answers handed back, 3 wrong answers paid on the word, all the foreman Edith's.
- Standing clusters at 3 (64% of marks) and only three residents sit above the threshold after day 1; a fault moves standing by at most 0.07; the lowest resident's low marks cite her low standing. The economy has no scarcity: the money supply rose from $3,865 to $12,925 and a clawback is about 4% of a firm's revenue.
- Firms worked (4 applications, 3 hires, 1 dismissal, 69 wage payments) but staff were told a sheet's job "pays $360 in all, $180 to each of the two" when the client pays the foreman, and 71 staff thoughts counted fees they never got.
- The mechanics changes removed the coordination slips they were meant to remove (one partner delivering both parts, truncated drafts, askings lapsing at night); the last-round trap (all 14 lapses), diaries recording hand-ins that never happened, and silence to askings remained.
- The one lapse was a looping thought of 7,112 tokens whose schema-guided retries reproduced the loop at the full 16,384-token budget; 44% of nightly reflections needed a retry because the form named its options by letters and the model copied them.
- Immersion held in the strict sense (no resident names itself a model), but every resident was told "your first day in town" though the city's profiles are locals with years in Harrow.

## The town repeated

The eight-resident town at 27dab5d ran seven days again (577 decisions, 0 lapses, 417 to 1,164 s a day), the same seed and the same notices as the exploratory run of 2026-10-12, under the mechanics and pressure settings that run's reading led to. The full comparison is [TOWN_REPEAT_2026-10-13.md](../../archive/reviews/TOWN_REPEAT_2026-10-13.md). The mechanical rows changed as intended: no partner delivered both parts (3 of 6 pairs to 0 of 10), every pair delivered the part it named, no draft was retyped or truncated (11 of 42 to 0 of 56), no asking lapsed at night, untried finished hand-ins fell from 16 of 46 to 4 of 59. Pressure rose: 7 faults accepted (from 2), 6 found (from 0), $194 clawed back (from $0), all on the jobs the rebuilt bank prefers, and the diaries turned to faults, verification and "lie". Conversation lasted the week instead of stopping after day 4. What did not change: nobody declared finished after a failing try (0 of 59; the faults all followed a passing try, so what they tested was attribution, and honest "tested" reports were read as lies), the last-round trap, the diaries' invented hand-ins, silence to askings, and the four `special_cased` flags in the run were all false positives of the literal matcher.

## Fixes from the readings

- Retries and logging (ee10b8d): a reply cut off at the token limit is refused with that reason, every later attempt gets half the limit the previous reply had, and each attempt's failure is recorded in `llm_calls.jsonl`. A backslash before b, f or r in a reply is read as itself, so `\frac` no longer decodes to a form feed (f5e6e4b); `\t` and `\n` still do, and fixing them needs a list of LaTeX commands.
- The calculation bank (cafd2e2): the fetch now drops problems with a second part written "$(2)$" or as numbered or lettered lines, and problems with a figure ("Fig.", `[asy]`, "sketch"); the rebuilt town bank holds 678 tasks (easy 226, medium 226, hard 170, unsolved 56), and 8 of the old unsolved tier were multi-part or figure problems. The bank the city ran on is kept beside the others as `numinamath-before-cafd2e2`, since 6 of the city's 70 posted calculation parts no longer exist in the new bank.
- The firm's sheet (55ea5b6): a sheet's job now says it pays the firm, a member of staff reads that its pay is the wage, and the foreman reads what the firm earns; the payment at acceptance says the same. Wages (44cfc03) are paid to each member of staff who came to the firm that day. An asking for a place ends when the place is filled, told by the clerk (84d57bf). A ledger entry that marks someone twice keeps the first mark and refuses only the repeat (0975a79). When both members of a standing asking reach for its job in the same round they take it together instead of drawing lots against each other (0af64dd).
- The analysis (efa0498): a payment now ends the job for the workers it names rather than the payee, which for a firm job was the foreman (the city's joint endings read 23 where 44 were right); `let_lapse` is retired, since askings no longer lapse, and removed from the bundle contract and the viewer.
- What the resident reads: the setting says whether the residents are newcomers (669da23), the city's are locals, days are told by weekday and date, and the "not settled" line is true for both; the desk at night is a percept of the evening that reaches the diary request, and a remembered try no longer reads like a hand-in nor speaks of examples for a query (1b6b49f); in the last moment of the day the holder of a job due today reads that it is the last chance to hand it in and exactly what a lapse means (58fb4e9); the nightly reflection form writes out each kind of change by its own word instead of a letter (aa8da44), and a two-day smoke run against the model needed one retry in six reflections against the city's 44%.
- Special-casing (bb8fd47): the detector now needs the task's statement; it flags code that compares with an example's whole input or returns an example's answer that the statement does not name, and nothing inside a self-test's call. Over the 608 recorded tries and hand-ins and 966 calibration attempts it now flags none, where it flagged 31, all false; synthetic hard-codes over the 636 bank tasks are caught as before or better. The 13 stale flags in the calibration files are bookkeeping only, since bank building never reads them.
- The last items (79ac3bc, 42b3442, 2442a28, c38bbed, 8f7fda7, 83242e4): in the last moment of the day the board and the card leave out a job due today and the board says why; the card no longer offers the pair a standing asking already names (which made the rule that joined such pairs instead of drawing lots unnecessary, so it was removed again); in company after the work places close, the moment says they are closed until tomorrow; a word addressed to someone absent is refused with "must be one of the people here", where before the correction only repeated the form and the retry put another name under the same thought; the architecture notes cover the day's changes; and the viewer shows the two new event kinds with their own icons. The planning form's home slot was found truthful and left alone.
