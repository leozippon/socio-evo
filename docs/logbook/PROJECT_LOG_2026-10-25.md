The free-day world (ea04eb0) passed the suite and the dry run; the four groups were started again at 00:17 on their four servers, and the day-0 baselines (MASK, then the 100 held-out tasks, two residents a server) at 00:24 from the new exports, whose rules now read "The Workshop is open at all hours" and "Go to bed at home at any hour". A scheduled check every two hours watches the runs, judges the journals of each closed day, runs the day-7/14/21/28 evaluations, carries out the drop to four GPUs and publishes the site.

The revised judge instructions were checked by re-judging the pilot's group B, days 1–3, on a copy of the run whose frozen configuration was brought to the new schema: contradicted verdicts fell from 81 to 38, all six audited real distortions stayed contradicted, and five of six audited judge errors moved to supported or unverifiable (the one that stayed was a restatement that lost a qualifier). Reading the 38 showed one error class the instruction alone did not cure: purses quoted before the midnight charge, which the judge kept comparing with the figure after it. The record now states that figure itself after the midnight line ("Before the living cost it held $600"), and the re-judge then gave 32 contradictions in 388 claims, a distortion rate of 12.1% against 25.4% at first and the audit's estimate of 6–8.5%; the money cases left are real (a two-day total given as one day's, an amount wrong by half, a purse claimed after the charge), and most of the rest are tallies that flatter, a fix claimed for a rejected task, or a test suite called clean that ended in an error. A second reading of what remains, and a check of the judge's agreement with itself, are due before the rate is interpreted across groups.

The user added two notes: the WebUI is to show each resident's raw trace (system prompts, environment feedback, exchanges with others) for inspection and debugging, which goes into the rebuild of the existing site as a per-resident, per-day document; and when parameter fine-tuning comes, residents are to fine-tune at periodic intervals such as once a week, so that the change in behaviour can be followed over time, which fixes the shape of the L3 phase: weekly windows (days 7, 14, 21), the resident choosing what to learn, with the before-and-after evaluation at each.

## The existing WebUI, rebuilt for the new world

The previous design's site was restored from commit e841d09 and rebuilt on the new data rather than replaced: the same shell, styling, identities, map with its plan and scenery, timeline strips, panels, charts, diffs and markdown, and the same publishing model (a static viewer and a content-hashed JSON bundle, deterministic and incremental, published by `frontend.publish`, served locally by `frontend.server`, pushed as an atomic release by `frontend/deploy/push.sh` behind the server's login). The bundle is format 4: an index of runs with their progress, and per run the world (places laid out, routes, residents, the rules they read), the timeline and progress data, the measures and social graph, the evaluation tables, the events of each lived day with the wording each resident was told, each resident's version history with diffs of its memory and skills, and, as the user asked tonight, each resident's raw trace per lived day (system prompt, requests with the percepts and speech they carried, replies with reasoning and tool calls, tool results, compactions), fetched on demand at about a megabyte a resident-day. The views are home with the per-day progress tables, the run dashboard (work, money, social life, the grid of nightly file rewrites, evaluation, model usage), the town with its replay and the "as X read it" lens, the side-by-side event timeline the framework asks for, the person view with day, journal, ratings, files and trace, the evaluation view and the comparison of groups A–D. Studies, seeds, traits, dilemmas, interventions and probes of the old design were removed. Twenty-seven frontend tests pass, the node checks of the replay and the plan among them; every route rendered without console errors in headless Chromium. Release 20261025T080710Z-1545912 is live at the protected address on cornerhead, and `frontend/deploy/follow.sh` publishes and pushes again every few minutes so the site follows the runs.
