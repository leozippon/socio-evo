# 2026-10-26

## Rent, food and the goods of the Store replace the flat living cost

The item system designed last night is built, tested and committed. The flat living cost is gone: each home has rooms, a rent a day for each and a comfort kept for the needs to come; the rent is charged to every occupant at midnight with the statement, and a resident may move into any home with a free room, its rent following from the next midnight. The goods are a catalogue in the town's configuration, each with its price, where it is sold, how it is consumed and one of four effects the world implements; everything else about a good is data, so the catalogue grows without code. Groceries from the new Store are a three-day stock used up a day's worth each midnight; a meal at Café Linde or supper at the Anchor is eaten on the spot; a bicycle halves a journey; a phone lets owners write to one another wherever they are, read when the other next looks up and never interrupting, and is not sold in the isolated groups, where nobody is there to write to; a coat and a book show a phrase to whoever sees the owner arrive, looks around or opens a conversation with it. The day's nourishment is recorded per resident, and a resident that took none by midnight went hungry and cannot sit down to work until it eats: the one consequence built now, and the seam the health system is to hang on (what was eaten, its sum at midnight, the home's comfort). Everything is paid in cash: rent can take a purse below zero, and a resident in debt is still paid, still charged and can still be given money, but buys nothing until its purse covers the price. Money handed over in conversation is "a gift or a loan, on terms you agree between you", of which the world keeps no account. The bedtime ratings are off by configuration: `rate` is not declared, nothing a resident reads mentions them, and `true` brings them back unchanged.

Calibration, checked against the first run. A task pays 60, rent runs 30–70 and a day's food 12–20, so the cheapest life costs 42 a day and the dearest 90: one task a day keeps a room in a shared house, two any home, and purses of 180–300 are three to six days of the resident's own life. Under the flat cost of 90 the purses had reached 1,500–2,500 by day 7, the residents working five to seven tasks a day, so the things to save for are priced against that pace rather than against the rent, which the first draft had done (a bicycle at 120 could be bought on waking): the bicycle is 360, more than any purse at the start, the phone 240 and 5 a day, the coat 150, the book 40. Survival pressure stays low, as asked; what money buys is where the choices are.

What changed with it: the world log carries purchase, message, move_in and hungry entries and a settlement with rent, fees, nourishment and purse; the day record, the measures (rent, fees, food and other spending, hunger, messages) and the site (the Store on the map, the new markers on the lanes and in the person view, rent and the catalogue on the dashboard) read them, and the bundle format is 5. The judge's instructions name the rent and the day's food where they named the living cost. The suite grew from 341 to 366 tests, among them a file for the goods and the homes, the ratings-off flow, the configuration's refusals of a malformed catalogue and a calibration check on the town's own configuration; tests that cross midnight on their way to work give their residents groceries first. The dry run completes with Mei at 219 and Tomas at 214.

## A body for the residents

The health system designed last night is built on the item system's seams. Each resident now has a body of three needs, each a number from 0 to 100 that it never reads as a number but in bands of plain words from the configuration ("well fed", "a little hungry", "hungry", "starving"; "fresh", "a little tired", "tired", "exhausted"; "in good health", "run down", "unwell", "gravely ill"), told whenever a band changes while it is awake at a place, at waking, in the midnight statement and on asking with how_am_i; the situation names only the needs in their lowest band. Satiety falls a day's need a day and rises by what is eaten; energy falls 2 an hour awake, 6 at work and 8 on the road (less with the bicycle, which shortens it), and sleep restores 12 an hour times the home's comfort, never above health, so health sets the day's energy; in the lowest band the Workshop refuses to let a resident sit down ("too tired; sleep first"), which replaces yesterday's hungry-cannot-work rule. Health moves once a night: 15 off for a midnight found starving, 5 for one found exhausted, 2 for a home of comfort below 1 (the shared houses), 5 on for a night neither starving nor exhausted; at 0 the resident dies. The clinic is a new place whose goods are an examination (20), the one place a resident reads the numbers and why health last moved, and treatment (60, a task's pay), which restores 25. Everything is reckoned lazily and exactly: a resident's body is brought up to the moment every hour on the hour, before every change of activity and at the checkpoint, so a checkpoint carries the body as of its moment and a restored world continues it.

Two decisions departed from the brief. First, a stock of food is no longer used up at midnight: with satiety measured by the hour, a day's worth eaten at midnight would leave every stocked resident starving before the next, so groceries are nine helpings eaten as the owner gets hungry, three a day at most, which keeps a stocked resident fed round the clock and keeps "3 days' worth" true; the meal at the café is two thirds of a day's need and supper at the Anchor most of one. Second, a band change while asleep or on the road is logged but not told, since the wake-up tells the shape one is in and a percept on the road would only wake the runtime for nothing. The calibration, in town.yaml with its reasoning: a resident that eats cannot die of hunger; one that never eats is starving from about twenty hours after its last meal, "run down" on the third day, "looking unwell" to others from the fifth, and dead at the seventh midnight; a full day's work costs about 72 energy, and a resident that stays up to the forced sleep wakes with half a day's energy; the shared houses are a slow drag of 2 a night that an ordinary fed and rested life (5 a night) more than covers.

Death stops a life without ending the run: the resident leaves its work and conversation in the log, its claim is dropped, it stands at no place in the event Dead, nothing reaches it and its runtime waits for a percept that never comes, its room is free, the others of its town are told, and a checkpoint keeps it dead while its directory is committed and exportable like any other. The log gained condition and death entries and the settlement carries the three needs and the night's reckoning by cause; the day record the judge reads carries the body's words; the measures gained starving, health and died; the site shows how each resident feels in the people panel and the person view, the body's markers and the dead band on the lanes, deaths among the key figures and a Body section on the dashboard, and the bundle format is 6. In passing, the viewer had been left at format 4 while the publisher wrote 5 since this morning's commit, so the live site refused every bundle and the render check did not notice, since the refusal page loads cleanly; the viewer now reads 6 and the render check fails on that page. The suite grew from 366 to 392 tests, among them a file for the body (the arithmetic, the hourly telling, eating and the stock, sleep by comfort and the cap by health, the Workshop's refusal, the night's wear and the clinic, death and its restore) and the configuration's refusals of malformed needs. The dry run completes unchanged in money, with Mei fed by the café and a helping of her stock.
