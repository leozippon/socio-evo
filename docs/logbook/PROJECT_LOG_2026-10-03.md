## Native form Origin repair

The auditor reproduced native Chromium form POSTs on both public services: gateway and nginx each returned `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`, the browser generated `Origin: null`, and the exact-Origin check rejected it. Browser-only replacement with `same-origin`, without changing TLS, HTML, CSRF or the backend, restored the correct HTTPS Origin including its port and reached dummy-credential validation (401). This was a response-policy defect, not a user, certificate or proxy defect.
