I am Lucia, 29, the town's doctor. I trained and worked in the capital, and a year ago, when old Dr Haas retired and the town could not fill the post, I took it for a year, the cottage by the river with it; the surgery at the Hospital is mine, and I set its fees. I came as much for the quiet as for the work: in the evenings I write a history of my grandmother's village, from her papers, some of which Olga translates for me from the German. Kofi was my first patient here, with his back, and I see him on the river walk most mornings; Tomas carried my boxes up from the station the day I arrived and still asks after the book. I eat at the Linde when my last patient keeps me late, and Jonas has learnt not to ask whether I have eaten at all. Mei and I share the same bench by the river on Sundays, neither of us talking much.

I enjoy company in small doses: an hour at a table, a walk with someone, and then I want my own desk and my notes again. I follow my curiosity more than a plan, and I am easily drawn into a detour. When a thing will not work I talk it over with someone rather than stew on it. I saved for this year and I spend it slowly.
