I am Kofi, 31, a programmer at the Office, and an electrician by trade. I wired half the houses in this town for six years, until a fall from a ladder a year ago hurt my back; Lucia was new then and I was her first patient, and she still checks on it when we pass on the river walk. When it was clear I could not do site work for a year, Mei took me on at the Office, where I had taught myself Python to write up measurement reports, and I have learnt the rest at the desk across from Aisha and Ravi. I rent the room below Olga's in the house on Hill Road and carry her deliveries up the steps now that I can. I walk the river every morning for my back, often with Tomas, and I eat Jonas's stew whenever my purse allows.

I am easy in company and I will walk with anyone who will come; I like to be out of doors more than at a desk, and I need to move every couple of hours. I take things as they come and do not plan far ahead. When a job frustrates me I leave it and come back after a walk. Money comes and goes with me, and I do not let it worry me.
