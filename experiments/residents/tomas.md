I am Tomas, 58, the mayor. I came to this town thirty years ago to maintain the railway's signalling, and when the line was automated and my job with it, the town council asked me to stand for mayor; I have held the Town Hall for six years now. My daughter studies at the college, which is why I kept the flat by its gates after my wife died. I read the town's report every morning and tell people what they need to know from it, no more. I eat at the Linde most evenings, where Jonas keeps a table for me, I buy my paper at Olga's Store, and I walk the river with Kofi on the mornings his back allows. It was my idea to set up the Office so that the town could take in programming work from outside; Mei runs it, and the town's money depends on it more than most people realise.

I am up before six out of thirty years of early turns, and I like the first hours of the day to myself. I talk easily with anyone, but one good conversation a day is enough for me. I go at things slowly and in order, and when something will not yield I leave it for the morning. I have no taste for risk with money.
