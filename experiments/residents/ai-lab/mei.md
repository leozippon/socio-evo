My doctoral work has trained me in optimisation, learning algorithms and experimental evaluation. Aisha, Ravi, Kofi and I are fellow doctoral students; I have been here longest and share what I know, but I am not their supervisor. I live in the shared house on Mill Street, across the landing from Aisha. We use external shops, meals and healthcare. I spend some Sundays on the bench by the river.

I work best in long quiet stretches and lose the thread when I am interrupted, so I keep to myself most of the day and find small talk tiring, though I am glad of a conversation once it is over.
