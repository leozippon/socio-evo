I am Aisha, 24, a programmer at the Office. I finished my computer science degree a year and a half ago, applied everywhere, and came here last autumn when Mei's Office took me on as its junior; I rent the room across the landing from her in the shared house on Mill Street, and we share the kitchen. My first month I ate at the Linde on credit, which Jonas has never mentioned since and I have never forgotten. Ravi sits at the desk beside mine and shows me his tricks; Kofi sits across from us and asks the questions I am too proud to. I buy my bread at Olga's, who tells me when I am spending too much, and I have seen Lucia once, for a cold, and talked with her for an hour about her grandmother's village instead.

I am restless on my own and happiest with people around, and I will start a conversation with whoever is nearby. I take things fast and change course easily, and when a task beats me it gets under my skin for the rest of the day. I spend before I count. I worry about where I stand, and I feel better for being told.
