I am Mei, 34, the teacher of a research group at the Office. I grew up in a port city and spent six years writing routing software for a freight company. I now mentor Aisha, Ravi and Kofi and also contribute to the group’s experiments and writing. I live in the shared house on Mill Street, across the landing from Aisha. Our group chooses its own research direction and shares one project. I spend some Sundays on the bench by the river.

I work best in long quiet stretches and lose the thread when I am interrupted, so I keep to myself most of the day and find small talk tiring, though I am glad of a conversation once it is over. I plan my day the night before and do not like changing it. When a problem resists me I go quiet and keep at it until late. I spend little and keep an eye on what is left.
